Dear Mamata,

On this beautiful Janmashtami, I came to give you a little blessing and a very special message. 💙

You know what makes my flute sound sweeter? A heart that loves purely. And your beautiful heart is one of those hearts that makes this world a little more beautiful. ✨

Just like Radha was special to me, there is someone who is very special in someone's heart...

And that someone is YOU, Mamata. ❤️

And may the love between two hearts always remain as beautiful as Radha and Krishna's eternal bond. 🦚💙

✨ Stay blessed, Mamata ✨

Happy Krishna Janmashtami ❤️
